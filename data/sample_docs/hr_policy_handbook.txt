HR Policy Handbook — Sample

Remote Work Policy:
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week, subject to manager approval.
Full-time remote arrangements require VP-level sign-off and are reviewed quarterly.

Vacation Policy:
Full-time employees accrue 18 vacation days per year, increasing to 22 days after 3 years
of service. Vacation requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance for approval.

Expense Reimbursement:
Employees are reimbursed for approved business expenses within 10 business days of
submitting a valid receipt via the internal expense portal.
